# Maple Pecan Brittle

## Ingredients

### Brittle Base
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup pure maple syrup
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 1/2 cups raw pecan halves
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 teaspoon sea salt

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Ingredients** Measure out all ingredients and have them ready near your cooking station.

2. **Create Syrup Base** In a heavy-bottomed saucepan, combine sugar, maple syrup, and water. Heat over medium-high, stirring until sugar dissolves completely.

3. **Cook Syrup** Stop stirring and let the mixture boil until it reaches 300°F (hard crack stage) on a candy thermometer, about 10-12 minutes.

4. **Add Nuts** Remove from heat and quickly stir in pecans, butter, vanilla, and sea salt until evenly coated.

5. **Set Brittle** Immediately pour onto a parchment-lined baking sheet, spreading thinly with a spatula. Let cool completely for 30-40 minutes.

6. **Break and Serve** Once hardened, break the brittle into irregular pieces and serve.
