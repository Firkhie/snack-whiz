# Caribbean Coconut Rum Balls

## Ingredients

### Truffle Base
- 2 cups shredded unsweetened coconut
- 1/2 cup sweetened condensed milk
- 1/4 cup dark rum
- 2 tablespoons ground almonds

### Coating
- 1/2 cup toasted coconut flakes
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg

## Instructions

1. **Toast Coconut** In a dry skillet, lightly toast shredded coconut until golden brown, stirring constantly to prevent burning.

2. **Mix Base** In a large bowl, combine toasted coconut, condensed milk, rum, and ground almonds. Mix thoroughly until a sticky dough forms.

3. **Prepare Coating** In a separate shallow dish, mix toasted coconut flakes with cinnamon and nutmeg.

4. **Shape Truffles** Using damp hands, roll the mixture into small 1-inch balls. 

5. **Coat Truffles** Roll each ball in the coconut-spice mixture, ensuring even coverage.

6. **Chill** Refrigerate for at least 1 hour to allow truffles to firm up and flavors to meld.

7. **Serve** Arrange on a serving platter and enjoy these tropical rum-infused treats.
