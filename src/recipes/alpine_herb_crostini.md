# Alpine Herb Crostini

## Ingredients

### Bread Base
- 1 baguette, thinly sliced
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil

### Herb Topping
- 2 tablespoons fresh thyme leaves
- 2 tablespoons fresh rosemary, finely chopped
- 3 tablespoons chives, minced
- 100g aged Gruyère cheese, finely grated
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- Sea salt and black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Baguette** Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C) and slice baguette into thin rounds, about 1/4 inch thick.

2. **Brush and Season** Arrange bread slices on a baking sheet, brush each with olive oil, and sprinkle with minced garlic.

3. **Add Herbs** Evenly distribute fresh thyme, rosemary, and chives over the bread slices.

4. **Cheese Topping** Sprinkle grated Gruyère cheese over each crostini, followed by a pinch of sea salt and black pepper.

5. **Bake** Place in oven and bake for 10-12 minutes until edges are golden and crisp and cheese is melted.

6. **Serve** Remove from oven and let cool for 2-3 minutes before serving as a crisp, herbaceous appetizer.
