# Caribbean Callaloo Croquettes

## Ingredients

### Croquette Base
- 2 cups chopped callaloo leaves
- 1 cup breadcrumbs
- 1/2 cup grated coconut
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1/4 cup finely chopped green onions
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 small scotch bonnet pepper, finely diced
- 1 tsp ground allspice
- Salt and black pepper to taste

### Coating
- 1 cup additional breadcrumbs
- 1/4 cup grated coconut

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Callaloo Mixture** Sauté callaloo, garlic, green onions, and scotch bonnet pepper in a pan until greens are wilted and moisture evaporates.

2. **Combine Ingredients** In a large bowl, mix cooked callaloo with breadcrumbs, coconut, eggs, allspice, salt, and pepper until well combined.

3. **Form Croquettes** Shape mixture into small oval croquettes, approximately 2 inches long.

4. **Coat Croquettes** Roll each croquette in additional breadcrumbs and coconut mixture until evenly covered.

5. **Fry Croquettes** Deep fry in hot oil at 350°F until golden brown and crispy, approximately 3-4 minutes.

6. **Drain and Serve** Remove croquettes, drain on paper towels, and serve hot with a spicy mango dipping sauce.
