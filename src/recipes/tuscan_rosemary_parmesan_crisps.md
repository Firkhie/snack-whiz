# Tuscan Rosemary Parmesan Crisps

## Ingredients

### Crisp Base
- 1 cup finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese
- 2 tablespoons fresh rosemary, finely chopped
- 1/4 teaspoon cracked black pepper
- 1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Cheese Mixture** Combine grated Parmigiano-Reggiano, chopped rosemary, and cracked black pepper in a mixing bowl.

2. **Form Crisps** Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Drop small tablespoon-sized mounds of cheese mixture, leaving 2 inches between each crisp.

3. **Bake** Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Bake for 5-7 minutes until edges are golden and crisp.

4. **Cool and Crisp** Let crisps cool completely on the baking sheet. They will become crunchier as they cool.

5. **Finish** Drizzle lightly with extra virgin olive oil before serving for an authentic Tuscan touch.
