# West African Peanut Plantain Bites

## Ingredients

### Plantain Chips
- 3 ripe plantains
- 2 tablespoons groundnut (peanut) powder
- 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1 teaspoon sea salt

### Dipping Sauce
- 1/4 cup roasted peanuts
- 2 tablespoons palm oil
- 1 small red chili, finely chopped
- 1 tablespoon fresh cilantro, chopped

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Plantains** Peel and slice plantains into thin, uniform chips using a mandoline or sharp knife.

2. **Season Chips** Mix groundnut powder, cayenne, ginger, and salt in a large bowl. Toss plantain slices in the spice mixture until evenly coated.

3. **Fry Chips** Heat palm oil in a deep skillet to 350°F. Fry plantain chips in batches until golden and crispy, about 2-3 minutes per batch.

4. **Make Dipping Sauce** Blend roasted peanuts, palm oil, chopped chili, and cilantro into a coarse sauce.

5. **Serve** Drain chips on paper towels and serve warm with peanut dipping sauce.
