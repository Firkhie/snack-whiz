# Provençal Herb Lavash Triangles

## Ingredients

### Lavash Base
- 2 large lavash flatbreads
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons dried herbes de Provence
- 1 teaspoon flaky sea salt
- 1/2 teaspoon cracked black pepper

### Optional Garnish
- Fresh thyme sprigs
- Grated Parmesan cheese

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Herbs** Combine herbes de Provence, olive oil, salt, and pepper in a small bowl to create a fragrant herb oil.

2. **Cut Lavash** Using kitchen scissors, cut lavash into triangular shapes approximately 2-3 inches wide.

3. **Season Triangles** Brush each lavash triangle generously with the herbed olive oil mixture, ensuring even coverage.

4. **Bake Crisps** Arrange triangles on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 8-10 minutes until edges are golden and crisp.

5. **Finish and Serve** Remove from oven, let cool for 2-3 minutes. Optionally sprinkle with additional Parmesan or fresh thyme before serving.
