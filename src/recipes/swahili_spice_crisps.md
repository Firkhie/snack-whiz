# Swahili Spice Crisps

## Ingredients

### Dough
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 3/4 cup warm water
- 2 tbsp olive oil

### Topping
- 2 tbsp dried dhania (coriander leaves)
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- 1 tsp ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Spice Dough** In a large bowl, mix flour, cumin, coriander, turmeric, and salt until well combined.

2. **Knead Dough** Gradually add warm water and olive oil, kneading until a smooth, elastic dough forms, about 8-10 minutes.

3. **Rest Dough** Cover the dough and let it rest for 20 minutes at room temperature.

4. **Roll and Cut** Divide dough into small portions, rolling each into thin, translucent rounds.

5. **Season and Bake** Sprinkle with dried dhania, sesame seeds, and black pepper. Bake at 375°F on a parchment-lined baking sheet for 12-15 minutes until crisp and golden.
