# Caribbean Coconut Jerk Bites

## Ingredients

### Chicken Bites
- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 2 tbsp jerk seasoning
- 1 cup shredded coconut
- 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
- 2 eggs, beaten
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Dipping Sauce
- 1/4 cup mango chutney
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped

## Instructions

1. **Marinate Chicken** Cut chicken thighs into bite-sized pieces and coat with jerk seasoning. Let marinate for 15 minutes.

2. **Prepare Coating** Mix shredded coconut and panko breadcrumbs in a shallow dish. Season with salt and pepper.

3. **Dredge Chicken** Dip each chicken piece in beaten eggs, then coat thoroughly in coconut-panko mixture.

4. **Bake Bites** Preheat oven to 400°F. Place coated chicken bites on a lined baking sheet. Bake for 20-25 minutes until golden and crispy.

5. **Prepare Sauce** While baking, mix mango chutney, lime juice, and cilantro in a small bowl.

6. **Serve** Remove bites from oven, let cool for 5 minutes. Serve hot with mango-cilantro dipping sauce.
