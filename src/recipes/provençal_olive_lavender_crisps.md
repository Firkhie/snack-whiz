# Provençal Olive Lavender Crisps

## Ingredients

### Dry Mix
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp dried lavender buds
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 1/2 tsp cracked black pepper

### Wet Ingredients
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 1/2 cup finely chopped sun-dried olives
- 2 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
- 1/3 cup cold water

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Dough** In a large mixing bowl, combine flour, lavender, salt, and pepper. Add olive oil, chopped olives, and thyme, mixing until crumbly.

2. **Knead and Rest** Gradually add cold water and knead into a smooth dough. Wrap in plastic and refrigerate for 30 minutes.

3. **Roll and Cut** Preheat oven to 375°F. Roll dough thinly on a floured surface and cut into thin rectangular crackers.

4. **Bake to Crisp** Place crackers on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Bake for 12-15 minutes until golden and crisp.

5. **Cool and Serve** Allow crackers to cool completely on a wire rack. Serve with soft goat cheese or as a standalone appetizer.
