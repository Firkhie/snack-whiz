# Levantine Sumac Sesame Crisps

## Ingredients

### Dry Ingredients
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 3 tablespoons ground sumac
- 2 tablespoons toasted sesame seeds
- 1 teaspoon sea salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

### Wet Ingredients
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1/2 cup warm water
- 1 egg white (for brushing)

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Dough** Mix flour, sumac, sesame seeds, salt, and pepper in a large bowl. Create a well in the center and add olive oil and warm water. Knead until a smooth dough forms.

2. **Rest Dough** Cover the dough and let it rest for 15 minutes at room temperature.

3. **Roll and Cut** Divide dough into small portions. Roll each portion very thin on a floured surface. Cut into diamond or triangular shapes.

4. **Bake Crisps** Preheat oven to 375°F. Brush crisps with egg white and sprinkle extra sumac and sesame seeds. Bake for 12-15 minutes until golden and crisp.

5. **Cool and Serve** Allow crisps to cool completely on a wire rack. Store in an airtight container.
