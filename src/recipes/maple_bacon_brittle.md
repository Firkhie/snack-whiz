# Maple Bacon Brittle

## Ingredients

### Brittle Base
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup pure maple syrup
- 1/4 cup water
- 1/2 teaspoon sea salt

### Mix-Ins
- 6 slices thick-cut bacon, crispy and crumbled
- 1/2 cup toasted pecans, roughly chopped
- 1 teaspoon black pepper

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Bacon** Cook bacon until extremely crisp, then drain and finely crumble.

2. **Create Syrup Mixture** In a heavy-bottomed saucepan, combine sugar, maple syrup, water, and salt. Heat over medium-high, stirring until sugar dissolves.

3. **Caramelize** Stop stirring and let mixture bubble until it reaches 300°F (hard crack stage) on a candy thermometer, about 12-15 minutes.

4. **Add Extras** Quickly stir in bacon bits, pecans, and black pepper. Immediately pour onto a silicone mat or buttered baking sheet.

5. **Cool and Break** Allow brittle to cool completely, then break into irregular pieces. Store in an airtight container.
