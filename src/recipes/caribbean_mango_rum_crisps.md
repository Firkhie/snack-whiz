# Caribbean Mango Rum Crisps

## Ingredients

### Crisp Base
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup cornmeal
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt

### Mango Rum Filling
- 2 ripe Caribbean mangoes, finely diced
- 3 tbsp dark rum
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Zest of 1 lime

### Coating
- 1/4 cup powdered sugar
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Mango Mixture** In a bowl, combine diced mangoes, rum, honey, vanilla, and lime zest. Let marinate for 15 minutes.

2. **Make Crisp Dough** Mix flour, cornmeal, brown sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl. Add marinated mango mixture and stir until a soft dough forms.

3. **Shape Crisps** Roll dough into small, thin rounds about 2 inches in diameter. Place on a parchment-lined baking sheet.

4. **Bake** Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Bake crisps for 15-18 minutes until golden and crispy around the edges.

5. **Finish** While warm, dust crisps with cinnamon-powdered sugar mixture. Let cool completely before serving.
