# Kenyan Safari Bites

## Ingredients

### Spice Mix
- 2 tbsp ground cumin
- 1 tbsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp ground cardamom
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper

### Base
- 2 cups cassava flour
- 1 cup ground roasted peanuts
- 1/4 cup finely chopped fresh cilantro
- 2 eggs
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Salt to taste

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Spice Blend** Mix all spices in a small bowl and set aside.

2. **Create Dough** In a large mixing bowl, combine cassava flour, ground peanuts, cilantro, and spice blend. Mix thoroughly.

3. **Bind Mixture** Add eggs and olive oil to the dry ingredients. Knead into a firm, slightly sticky dough.

4. **Shape Bites** Roll dough into small, bite-sized balls, about 1 inch in diameter.

5. **Bake** Preheat oven to 375°F. Place bites on a lined baking sheet and bake for 20-25 minutes until golden and crisp.

6. **Serve** Allow to cool for 5 minutes. Serve warm with a side of tangy mango chutney or yogurt dip.
