# Caribbean Coconut Calypso Crisps

## Ingredients

### Coconut Base
- 2 large fresh coconuts
- 3 tablespoons dark rum
- 1 tablespoon lime zest
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon sea salt

### Spice Blend
- 1/4 teaspoon ground allspice
- 1/8 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Coconut** Carefully crack open coconuts and remove fresh coconut meat in large pieces.

2. **Slice Coconut** Using a mandoline or sharp knife, slice coconut meat into thin, uniform chips.

3. **Marinate** In a large bowl, combine rum, lime zest, brown sugar, sea salt, allspice, cayenne, and thyme. Toss coconut chips in mixture.

4. **Dry Chips** Spread marinated coconut chips on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper in a single layer.

5. **Bake and Crisp** Bake at 325°F for 15-20 minutes, turning once halfway through, until edges are golden and crisp.

6. **Cool and Serve** Allow chips to cool completely, which will further crisp them. Store in an airtight container.
