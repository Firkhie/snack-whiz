# Baklava Bites

## Ingredients

### Filling
- 1 cup chopped walnuts
- 1/2 cup chopped pistachios
- 1/2 cup brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cloves

### Pastry
- 8 sheets phyllo dough
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted

### Honey Drizzle
- 1/4 cup honey

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the Filling** In a medium bowl, mix together the walnuts, pistachios, brown sugar, cinnamon, and cloves. Set aside.

2. **Assemble the Baklava Bites** Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9x13 inch baking dish. Lay one sheet of phyllo dough in the prepared baking dish and brush with melted butter. Repeat with 7 more sheets of phyllo, brushing each layer with butter.

3. **Spread the Filling** Evenly spread the nut mixture over the phyllo layers.

4. **Top with Phyllo** Place the remaining 8 sheets of phyllo dough on top, brushing each layer with melted butter.

5. **Bake the Baklava** Bake for 35-40 minutes, or until the phyllo is golden brown and crispy.

6. **Drizzle with Honey** Remove the baklava from the oven and immediately drizzle the honey over the top. Allow to cool completely before cutting into small squares or triangles.
