# Patagonian Yerba Mate Energy Bites

## Ingredients

### Dry Ingredients
- 1 cup rolled quinoa
- 1/2 cup ground almonds
- 1/4 cup ground yerba mate leaves
- 2 tablespoons chia seeds
- 1/4 cup cocoa nibs

### Wet Ingredients
- 1/3 cup honey
- 1/4 cup almond butter
- 2 tablespoons dark chocolate, melted
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Dry Mix** In a large bowl, combine rolled quinoa, ground almonds, yerba mate leaves, chia seeds, and cocoa nibs.

2. **Combine Wet Ingredients** In a separate bowl, whisk together honey, almond butter, melted dark chocolate, and vanilla extract until smooth.

3. **Mix and Form** Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients, stirring until a cohesive mixture forms. If too dry, add a little more honey.

4. **Shape Bites** Using clean hands, roll mixture into small 1-inch balls. Place on a parchment-lined tray.

5. **Chill** Refrigerate energy bites for at least 1 hour to set. Store in an airtight container for up to a week.
