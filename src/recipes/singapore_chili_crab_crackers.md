# Singapore Chili Crab Crackers

## Ingredients

### Crackers
- 2 cups tapioca flour
- 1/2 cup crab meat, finely shredded
- 1 egg white
- 2 tbsp chili sauce
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Salt to taste

### Dipping Sauce
- 3 tbsp tomato ketchup
- 2 tbsp sambal oelek
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tsp ginger, minced

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Cracker Dough** Mix tapioca flour, shredded crab meat, egg white, chili sauce, garlic powder, and salt in a bowl until a smooth dough forms.

2. **Shape Crackers** Roll the dough into thin sheets and cut into small triangular or rectangular shapes.

3. **Dry Crackers** Leave the cut crackers on a baking sheet to air dry for 30 minutes.

4. **Fry Crackers** Deep fry in hot oil at 350°F until they puff up and turn golden brown, about 2-3 minutes.

5. **Prepare Sauce** Whisk together all dipping sauce ingredients until well combined.

6. **Serve** Drain crackers on paper towels and serve hot with the spicy dipping sauce.
