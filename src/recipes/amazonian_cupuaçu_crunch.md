# Amazonian Cupuaçu Crunch

## Ingredients

### Crunch Base
- 1 cup cassava flour
- 1/2 cup ground Brazil nuts
- 1/4 cup cupuaçu powder
- 2 tablespoons native honey
- 1 egg
- 1/2 teaspoon sea salt

### Topping
- 2 tablespoons raw cacao nibs
- 1 tablespoon dried açaí powder

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Dough** Mix cassava flour, ground Brazil nuts, cupuaçu powder, and sea salt in a large bowl.

2. **Bind Ingredients** Add egg and honey, kneading until a cohesive dough forms.

3. **Shape Crisps** Roll dough thin on parchment paper, cut into triangular shapes.

4. **Bake** Spread on baking sheet, bake at 375°F for 20-25 minutes until golden and crisp.

5. **Finish** Sprinkle with cacao nibs and açaí powder while still warm.

6. **Cool and Serve** Let crisps cool completely before serving, allowing them to become extra crispy.
