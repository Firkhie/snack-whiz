# Caribbean Rum Raisin Coconut Squares

## Ingredients

### Raisin Mixture
- 1 cup golden raisins
- 1/4 cup dark rum
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar

### Coconut Base
- 2 cups shredded coconut
- 1/3 cup sweetened condensed milk
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1/4 teaspoon sea salt

### Coating
- 1/2 cup toasted coconut flakes
- 2 tablespoons powdered sugar

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Raisins** Soak raisins in rum for 30 minutes, allowing them to plump and absorb the liquid.

2. **Create Coconut Mixture** In a large skillet, toast shredded coconut until golden brown. Remove from heat and mix with condensed milk, melted butter, vanilla, nutmeg, and salt.

3. **Combine Ingredients** Fold rum-soaked raisins into coconut mixture, ensuring even distribution.

4. **Form Squares** Press mixture into a lined 8x8 inch baking pan, creating an even layer approximately 1/2 inch thick.

5. **Chill and Set** Refrigerate for 1 hour until firm.

6. **Finish and Serve** Cut into small squares, roll edges in toasted coconut flakes and dust lightly with powdered sugar before serving.
