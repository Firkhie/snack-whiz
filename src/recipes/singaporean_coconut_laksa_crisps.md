# Singaporean Coconut Laksa Crisps

## Ingredients

### Crisp Base
- 2 cups rice flour
- 1/2 cup tapioca starch
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup coconut milk
- 1/2 cup water

### Laksa Seasoning
- 2 tbsp laksa paste
- 1 tbsp dried shrimp powder
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp ground lemongrass
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes

### Garnish
- Dried kaffir lime leaves, finely crushed
- Toasted coconut flakes

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Dough** Mix rice flour, tapioca starch, and salt in a large bowl. Gradually add coconut milk and water, kneading until a smooth, pliable dough forms.

2. **Flavor Infusion** Incorporate laksa paste, shrimp powder, turmeric, lemongrass, and chili flakes into the dough, ensuring even distribution.

3. **Roll and Cut** Roll dough thinly on a floured surface and cut into small triangular or rectangular shapes.

4. **Dry Crackers** Let cut dough pieces air dry for 30 minutes to develop a crisp texture.

5. **Fry to Perfection** Deep fry in hot oil at 350°F until golden and crispy, about 2-3 minutes.

6. **Finish and Garnish** Drain on paper towels and sprinkle with crushed kaffir lime leaves and toasted coconut flakes while still warm.
