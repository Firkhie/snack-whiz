# Amazonian Açaí Berry Crackers

## Ingredients

### Cracker Base
- 1 cup quinoa flour
- 1/2 cup ground Brazil nuts
- 1/4 cup dried açaí berry powder
- 2 tablespoons chia seeds
- 1 teaspoon sea salt

### Topping
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon fresh lime zest
- 2 tablespoons crushed dried açaí berries

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Dry Ingredients** Mix quinoa flour, ground Brazil nuts, açaí powder, chia seeds, and sea salt in a large mixing bowl until well combined.

2. **Form Dough** Add water gradually, mixing until the ingredients form a cohesive, slightly sticky dough that can be rolled out.

3. **Roll and Cut** On a parchment-lined baking sheet, roll the dough very thin (about 1/8 inch thick) and cut into small rectangular crackers.

4. **Season Crackers** Brush crackers with olive oil and sprinkle with lime zest and crushed dried açaí berries.

5. **Bake** Bake at 375°F for 15-18 minutes, or until edges are crisp and golden brown. Allow to cool completely before serving.
