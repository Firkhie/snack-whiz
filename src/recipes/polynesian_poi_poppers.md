# Polynesian Poi Poppers

## Ingredients

### Fritter Base
- 2 cups taro root, peeled and grated
- 1/2 cup rice flour
- 1/4 cup coconut milk
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 1/2 tsp ground white pepper

### Coating
- 1/2 cup shredded coconut
- 1/4 cup panko breadcrumbs

### Passionfruit Glaze
- 1/4 cup passionfruit pulp
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp lime juice

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Taro Base** Grate taro root and mix with rice flour, coconut milk, egg, salt, and pepper to form a thick batter.

2. **Form Poppers** Using wet hands, shape mixture into small round balls about 1-inch in diameter.

3. **Coat Poppers** Roll each ball in a mixture of shredded coconut and panko breadcrumbs.

4. **Fry Poppers** Deep fry in vegetable oil at 350°F for 3-4 minutes until golden brown and crispy.

5. **Make Glaze** Whisk passionfruit pulp, honey, and lime juice in a small bowl until smooth.

6. **Serve** Drain poppers on paper towels and drizzle with passionfruit glaze. Serve warm.
