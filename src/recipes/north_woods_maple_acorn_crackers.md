# North Woods Maple Acorn Crackers

## Ingredients

### Dry Ingredients
- 1 cup acorn flour
- 1 cup all-purpose wheat flour
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 1/2 tsp dried thyme
- 1/4 tsp ground black pepper

### Wet Ingredients
- 1/4 cup pure maple syrup
- 3 tbsp cold pressed sunflower oil
- 2 tbsp water

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Dry Mix** In a large mixing bowl, combine acorn flour, wheat flour, salt, thyme, and black pepper. Whisk thoroughly to distribute ingredients evenly.

2. **Create Dough** Add maple syrup, sunflower oil, and water to dry ingredients. Mix with a wooden spoon until a cohesive dough forms.

3. **Roll and Cut** On a lightly floured surface, roll dough to 1/8 inch thickness. Cut into small rectangular crackers using a sharp knife or pastry wheel.

4. **Bake Crackers** Preheat oven to 375°F. Place crackers on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Bake for 12-15 minutes until edges are golden brown and crisp.

5. **Cool and Serve** Remove from oven and let crackers cool completely on a wire rack. Serve with soft cheese or enjoy as a standalone snack.
