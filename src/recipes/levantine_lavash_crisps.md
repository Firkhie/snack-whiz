# Levantine Lavash Crisps

## Ingredients

### Crisps
- 2 large sheets of lavash bread
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons za'atar spice blend
- 1 tablespoon ground sumac
- 2 tablespoons toasted sesame seeds
- Sea salt to taste

### Optional Dipping Sauce
- 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 tablespoon fresh mint, finely chopped
- 1 clove garlic, minced

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Ingredients** Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

2. **Cut Lavash** Carefully cut lavash bread into triangular or rectangular shapes for even crisping.

3. **Season Crisps** Brush lavash pieces with olive oil and generously sprinkle za'atar, sumac, sesame seeds, and sea salt.

4. **Bake** Arrange seasoned lavash on baking sheet and bake for 8-10 minutes until edges are golden and crispy.

5. **Prepare Dip** While crisps cool, mix yogurt with mint and garlic for an optional complementary dipping sauce.

6. **Serve** Allow crisps to cool completely before serving with yogurt dip or enjoying on their own.
