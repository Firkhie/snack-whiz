```markdown
# Caribbean Papaya Pepper Poppers

## Ingredients

### Fritter Base
- 2 ripe papayas, diced
- 1 small scotch bonnet pepper, finely minced
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup green onions, chopped
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

### Coating
- 1 cup breadcrumbs
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1/2 tsp ground allspice

### Dipping Sauce
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 2 tbsp fresh lime juice
- 1 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Papaya Mixture** In a large bowl, mash diced papaya and mix with minced scotch bonnet pepper, eggs, green onions, salt, and black pepper until well combined.

2. **Form Fritter Batter** Gradually fold in flour until a thick, slightly sticky batter forms.

3. **Create Coating** In a separate shallow dish, mix breadcrumbs, thyme, and allspice.

4. **Shape Poppers** Using a spoon, drop small portions of papaya mixture into breadcrumb mixture, rolling to coat evenly.

5. **Fry Poppers** Heat oil to 350°F and deep fry poppers in batches until golden brown, about 3-4 minutes.

6. **Prepare Dipping Sauce** Whisk sour cream, lime juice, and cilantro together in a small bowl.

7. **Serve** Drain poppers on paper towels and serve hot with cilantro-lime dipping sauce.
```
