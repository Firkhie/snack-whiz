# Singaporean Satay Crisp Rolls

## Ingredients

### Filling
- 2 chicken breasts, finely diced
- 2 tbsp satay spice blend
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- 2 green onions, minced

### Crisp Components
- Rice paper wrappers
- 1/2 cup roasted peanuts, crushed
- 2 tbsp sesame seeds
- Vegetable oil for frying

### Dipping Sauce
- 1/4 cup peanut sauce
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tsp chili oil

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Chicken** Marinate diced chicken in satay spice blend for 30 minutes.

2. **Cook Chicken** Sauté marinated chicken until golden and fully cooked, then cool.

3. **Assemble Rolls** Soften rice paper, add chicken, cilantro, and green onions. Roll tightly.

4. **Create Crisp Coating** Mix crushed peanuts and sesame seeds on a flat plate.

5. **Fry Rolls** Gently roll prepared rolls in peanut-sesame mixture, then shallow fry until crispy and golden.

6. **Serve** Cut rolls diagonally and serve with spicy peanut dipping sauce.
