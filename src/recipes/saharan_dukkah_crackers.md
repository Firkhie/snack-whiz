# Saharan Dukkah Crackers

## Ingredients

### Cracker Base
- 1 cup whole wheat flour
- 1/2 cup ground almonds
- 2 tablespoons sesame seeds
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1/3 cup water

### Dukkah Spice Blend
- 1/4 cup toasted hazelnuts
- 2 tablespoons coriander seeds
- 1 tablespoon cumin seeds
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon sea salt

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Spice Blend** Toast nuts and seeds in a dry skillet until fragrant, then grind coarsely in a spice grinder.

2. **Make Dough** Mix flour, ground almonds, sesame seeds, and salt in a large bowl. Add olive oil and water, kneading until a smooth dough forms.

3. **Roll and Cut** Roll dough thin on a floured surface, sprinkle with half the dukkah blend, and cut into triangular shapes.

4. **Bake Crackers** Place on a baking sheet, sprinkle remaining dukkah on top, and bake at 375°F for 15-18 minutes until golden and crisp.

5. **Cool and Serve** Let crackers cool completely, then serve with hummus or enjoy as a standalone snack.
