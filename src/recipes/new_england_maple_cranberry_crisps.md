# New England Maple Cranberry Crisps

## Ingredients

### Crisp Base
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/4 cup pure maple syrup
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
- 1/4 cup dried cranberries, finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 teaspoon sea salt

### Maple Glaze
- 2 tablespoons maple syrup
- 1 tablespoon powdered sugar
- Pinch of cinnamon

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Dough** Mix flour, oats, maple syrup, melted butter, chopped cranberries, vanilla, and salt in a large bowl until a cohesive dough forms.

2. **Roll and Cut** Roll dough to 1/8-inch thickness and cut into thin rectangular crackers. Place on a parchment-lined baking sheet.

3. **Bake Crisps** Bake at 375°F for 12-15 minutes until edges are golden brown and crisp.

4. **Glaze and Finish** Whisk maple glaze ingredients and lightly brush over warm crisps. Let cool completely to allow glaze to set.
