# Ethiopian Berbere Bites

## Ingredients

### Dry Ingredients
- 1 cup teff flour
- 1/2 cup red lentil flour
- 2 tablespoons berbere spice blend
- 1 teaspoon sea salt
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder

### Wet Ingredients
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1/3 cup water
- 1 egg, beaten

### Garnish
- Toasted sesame seeds
- Dried ground chili flakes

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Dough** Mix dry ingredients in a large bowl, then gradually add wet ingredients to form a smooth, cohesive dough.

2. **Rest Dough** Cover and let dough rest for 20 minutes at room temperature to allow flours to hydrate.

3. **Roll and Cut** Roll dough very thin on a floured surface and cut into small triangular shapes.

4. **Bake** Arrange on a baking sheet and bake at 375°F for 12-15 minutes until crisp and golden brown.

5. **Finish** Sprinkle with sesame seeds and extra chili flakes while still warm. Cool completely before serving.
