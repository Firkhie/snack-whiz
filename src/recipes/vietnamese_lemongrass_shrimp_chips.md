# Vietnamese Lemongrass Shrimp Chips

## Ingredients

### Shrimp Mixture
- 200g fresh shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 3 stalks fresh lemongrass, finely minced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tablespoon fish sauce
- 1 teaspoon palm sugar

### Cracker Base
- 250g rice flour
- 50g tapioca starch
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 150ml warm water
- Vegetable oil for deep frying

### Dipping Sauce
- 3 tablespoons lime juice
- 1 tablespoon fish sauce
- 1 red chili, finely chopped
- 1 tablespoon fresh cilantro, chopped

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Shrimp Mixture** Finely chop shrimp and mix with lemongrass, garlic, fish sauce, and palm sugar. Let marinate for 15 minutes.

2. **Make Cracker Dough** Combine rice flour, tapioca starch, and salt. Gradually add warm water and knead into a smooth dough. Let rest for 10 minutes.

3. **Incorporate Shrimp** Fold the marinated shrimp mixture into the dough until evenly distributed.

4. **Shape Crackers** Roll dough into thin sheets and cut into triangular shapes.

5. **Fry Chips** Heat oil to 375°F and deep fry crackers until golden and crispy, about 2-3 minutes.

6. **Prepare Dipping Sauce** Mix all sauce ingredients in a small bowl.

7. **Serve** Drain chips on paper towels and serve hot with dipping sauce.
