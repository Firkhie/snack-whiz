# Caribbean Tamarind Mango Fritters

## Ingredients

### Fritter Base
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup cornmeal
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 large egg
- 3/4 cup milk

### Fruit Filling
- 1 ripe mango, diced
- 2 tbsp tamarind paste
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon

### Frying
- Vegetable oil for deep frying
- Powdered sugar for dusting

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Batter** In a large bowl, mix flour, cornmeal, baking powder, and salt. Whisk in egg and milk until smooth.

2. **Make Fruit Mixture** In a separate bowl, combine diced mango, tamarind paste, brown sugar, and cinnamon. Gently fold this mixture into the batter.

3. **Heat Oil** Fill a deep skillet with 2 inches of vegetable oil and heat to 350°F (175°C).

4. **Fry Fritters** Drop spoonfuls of batter into hot oil, frying until golden brown, about 2-3 minutes per side.

5. **Drain and Serve** Remove fritters with a slotted spoon, drain on paper towels, and dust with powdered sugar while still warm.
