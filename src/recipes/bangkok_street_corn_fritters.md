# Bangkok Street Corn Fritters

## Ingredients

### Fritters
- 3 ears fresh corn, kernels removed
- 1/2 cup rice flour
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 green onions, finely chopped
- 1 red Thai chili, minced
- 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped
- 1 egg
- 1/4 cup coconut milk
- 1 teaspoon fish sauce
- Salt and white pepper to taste
- Vegetable oil for frying

### Chili Dipping Sauce
- 3 tablespoons sweet chili sauce
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- 1 teaspoon grated ginger
- Chopped cilantro for garnish

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Batter** In a large bowl, mix corn kernels, rice flour, all-purpose flour, green onions, Thai chili, and cilantro.

2. **Combine Wet Ingredients** Whisk egg, coconut milk, and fish sauce together, then fold into the corn mixture until well combined.

3. **Heat Oil** Heat vegetable oil in a deep skillet to 350°F (175°C).

4. **Fry Fritters** Drop spoonfuls of batter into hot oil, frying until golden brown and crispy, about 2-3 minutes per side.

5. **Drain and Season** Remove fritters with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels. Sprinkle with salt while hot.

6. **Prepare Sauce** Mix sweet chili sauce, lime juice, and ginger in a small bowl.

7. **Serve** Plate fritters with dipping sauce and garnish with extra cilantro. Enjoy hot and crispy!
