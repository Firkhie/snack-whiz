# Oceanian Kava Root Crunch

## Ingredients

### Dry Ingredients
- 1 cup taro flour
- 1/2 cup coconut flour
- 2 tbsp ground kava root powder
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 1/4 tsp ground turmeric

### Wet Ingredients
- 1/3 cup coconut milk
- 2 tbsp coconut oil
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp honey

### Topping
- Toasted coconut flakes
- Pinch of ground cinnamon

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Dry Mix** Combine taro flour, coconut flour, kava root powder, salt, and turmeric in a large mixing bowl.

2. **Create Wet Mixture** In a separate bowl, whisk together coconut milk, melted coconut oil, egg, and honey until well blended.

3. **Combine Ingredients** Gradually add wet ingredients to dry ingredients, mixing until a firm dough forms.

4. **Roll and Cut** On a floured surface, roll dough to 1/8-inch thickness and cut into triangular shapes.

5. **Bake Crisps** Place on a parchment-lined baking sheet and bake at 375°F for 20-25 minutes until golden and crisp.

6. **Finish** Sprinkle with toasted coconut flakes and cinnamon while still warm. Allow to cool completely before serving.
