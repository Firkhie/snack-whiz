# Persian Pistachio Poppers

## Ingredients

### Pastry Base
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup semolina flour
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1/4 cup warm water
- 1 tsp rose water
- 1/2 tsp ground cardamom

### Filling
- 1 cup ground pistachios
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp orange blossom water
- Pinch of sea salt

### Coating
- 1/2 cup finely chopped pistachios
- 2 tbsp powdered sugar

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Dough** Mix flours, olive oil, warm water, rose water, and cardamom until a smooth dough forms. Let rest for 30 minutes.

2. **Create Filling** Combine ground pistachios, honey, orange blossom water, and salt in a bowl until well mixed.

3. **Shape Poppers** Roll dough thin, cut into small circles. Place a small amount of pistachio filling in the center of each circle.

4. **Fold and Seal** Fold dough over filling to create small crescent shapes. Crimp edges with a fork to seal.

5. **Fry Poppers** Heat oil to 350°F and deep fry poppers until golden brown, about 2-3 minutes.

6. **Finish and Garnish** Drain on paper towels, then roll warm poppers in chopped pistachios and powdered sugar.
