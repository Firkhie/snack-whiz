# Caribbean Guava Sunshine Rolls

## Ingredients

### Pastry
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup unsalted butter, cold and cubed
- 2 tablespoons white rum
- Pinch of salt

### Filling
- 1 cup fresh guava, diced
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 tablespoon lime zest

### Glaze
- 1/4 cup powdered sugar
- 2 tablespoons coconut milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Pastry Dough** Mix flour and salt, then cut in cold butter until crumbly. Add rum and knead into a smooth dough. Refrigerate for 30 minutes.

2. **Make Guava Filling** Combine diced guava, brown sugar, cinnamon, and lime zest in a small saucepan. Cook over medium heat for 10 minutes until slightly thickened.

3. **Assemble Rolls** Roll out chilled dough into a thin rectangle. Spread guava filling, then roll tightly and slice into 1-inch pieces.

4. **Bake** Place rolls on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Bake at 375°F for 15-18 minutes until golden brown.

5. **Finish with Glaze** Mix powdered sugar, coconut milk, and vanilla. Drizzle over warm rolls before serving.
