# Great Lakes Wild Rice Crisps

## Ingredients

### Dry Ingredients
- 1 cup cooked wild rice
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup cornmeal
- 2 tablespoons maple sugar
- 1 teaspoon sea salt
- 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme

### Wet Ingredients
- 1/4 cup maple syrup
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/4 cup dried cranberries, finely chopped
- 1 large egg

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Mixture** In a large bowl, combine all dry ingredients and mix thoroughly.

2. **Create Dough** Add wet ingredients to dry ingredients, stirring until a cohesive dough forms.

3. **Roll and Cut** On a floured surface, roll dough to 1/8-inch thickness and cut into small triangles or rectangles.

4. **Bake Crisps** Place crackers on a parchment-lined baking sheet and bake at 375°F for 20-25 minutes until golden and crisp.

5. **Cool and Serve** Allow crisps to cool completely before serving, which will enhance their crunchiness.
