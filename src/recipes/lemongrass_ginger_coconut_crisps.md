# Lemongrass Ginger Coconut Crisps

## Ingredients

### Crisp Base
- 2 cups rice flour
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemongrass, finely minced
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1/2 teaspoon sea salt

### Coating
- 1 cup shredded unsweetened coconut
- 2 tablespoons coconut oil
- 1 teaspoon palm sugar

### Dipping Sauce
- 3 tablespoons sweet chili sauce
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- Fresh cilantro, chopped

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Dough** Mix rice flour, lemongrass, ginger, and salt in a large bowl. Gradually add warm water to form a smooth, pliable dough.

2. **Roll and Cut** Roll dough thinly on a floured surface and cut into small triangles or rectangles.

3. **Toast Coconut** In a dry skillet, toast shredded coconut until golden brown, stirring constantly.

4. **Fry Crisps** Deep fry dough pieces in hot oil until golden and crispy, about 2-3 minutes.

5. **Coat and Finish** Toss hot crisps in melted coconut oil, then roll in toasted coconut and palm sugar mixture.

6. **Serve** Arrange on a platter with sweet chili lime dipping sauce, garnished with fresh cilantro.
