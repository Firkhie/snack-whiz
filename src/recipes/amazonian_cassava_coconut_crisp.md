# Amazonian Cassava Coconut Crisp

## Ingredients

### Dry Ingredients
- 1½ cups cassava flour
- ½ cup unsweetened shredded coconut
- 2 tablespoons ground chia seeds
- 1 teaspoon sea salt

### Wet Ingredients
- ¼ cup coconut milk
- 2 tablespoons organic honey
- 1 large egg
- 2 tablespoons coconut oil, melted

### Topping
- Extra shredded coconut
- Pinch of ground cinnamon

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Dry Mix** Combine cassava flour, shredded coconut, ground chia seeds, and sea salt in a large mixing bowl.

2. **Create Wet Blend** In a separate bowl, whisk together coconut milk, honey, egg, and melted coconut oil until smooth.

3. **Combine Ingredients** Gradually fold wet ingredients into dry ingredients, mixing until a cohesive dough forms.

4. **Shape Crisps** Roll dough between parchment paper to ⅛-inch thickness and cut into triangular shapes.

5. **Garnish** Sprinkle additional shredded coconut and cinnamon over cut shapes.

6. **Bake** Arrange on a baking sheet and cook at 375°F for 15-18 minutes until edges are golden and crisp.

7. **Cool and Serve** Let crisps cool completely on a wire rack to maintain crunchiness.
