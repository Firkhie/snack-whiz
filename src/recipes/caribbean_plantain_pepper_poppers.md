# Caribbean Plantain Pepper Poppers

## Ingredients

### Poppers
- 2 ripe plantains
- 1/2 cup soft goat cheese
- 2 scotch bonnet peppers, finely minced
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 2 green onions, thinly sliced
- Salt and black pepper to taste

### Coating
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- Vegetable oil for frying

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Filling** Mix goat cheese, minced scotch bonnet peppers, cilantro, green onions, salt, and black pepper in a bowl until well combined.

2. **Prepare Plantains** Peel plantains and slice lengthwise into 1/4-inch thick strips. Cut each strip into 2-3 inch segments.

3. **Stuff Poppers** Create a small pocket in each plantain segment and carefully fill with cheese mixture.

4. **Coat Poppers** Dredge each stuffed plantain segment first in flour, then egg wash, and finally coat with seasoned panko breadcrumbs.

5. **Fry Poppers** Heat oil to 350°F and fry poppers in batches until golden brown and crispy, about 2-3 minutes per side.

6. **Drain and Serve** Remove poppers with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels. Serve hot with a cooling mango or lime dipping sauce.
