# Caribbean Passion Fruit Coconut Clusters

## Ingredients

### Clusters
- 1 cup shredded unsweetened coconut
- 1/2 cup passion fruit pulp
- 1/4 cup honey
- 2 tablespoons rum (optional)
- 1/4 teaspoon sea salt
- 1/4 cup toasted macadamia nuts, chopped

### Coating
- 1/2 cup powdered sugar
- 2 tablespoons dried coconut flakes

## Instructions

1. **Toast Coconut** In a dry skillet, lightly toast shredded coconut until golden brown, stirring constantly to prevent burning.

2. **Mix Wet Ingredients** In a medium bowl, combine passion fruit pulp, honey, rum, and sea salt until well blended.

3. **Combine Mixture** Add toasted coconut and chopped macadamia nuts to the wet ingredients, mixing thoroughly until a sticky consistency forms.

4. **Shape Clusters** Using damp hands, roll mixture into small 1-inch balls, placing them on a parchment-lined baking sheet.

5. **Coat and Chill** Roll clusters in powdered sugar and dried coconut flakes, then refrigerate for 30 minutes to set.

6. **Serve** Store in an airtight container and enjoy these tropical, tangy-sweet clusters chilled or at room temperature.
