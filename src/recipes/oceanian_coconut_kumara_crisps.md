# Oceanian Coconut Kumara Crisps

## Ingredients

### Crisps
- 2 large orange kumara (sweet potatoes)
- 1 cup shredded unsweetened coconut
- 2 tablespoons macadamia nut oil
- 1 teaspoon sea salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon

### Dipping Sauce
- 1/4 cup coconut cream
- 1 tablespoon honey
- Pinch of ground ginger

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Kumara** Peel and slice kumara into thin, uniform rounds using a mandoline or sharp knife.

2. **Season and Coat** Toss kumara slices with macadamia nut oil, sea salt, and cinnamon. Ensure each slice is evenly coated.

3. **Add Coconut** Sprinkle shredded coconut over the seasoned kumara slices, gently pressing to help coconut adhere.

4. **Bake Crisps** Arrange slices on a parchment-lined baking sheet in a single layer. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25-30 minutes, turning halfway, until edges are golden and crisp.

5. **Prepare Dipping Sauce** While crisps bake, whisk coconut cream, honey, and ginger in a small bowl until smooth.

6. **Serve** Allow crisps to cool for 5 minutes. Serve warm with coconut honey dipping sauce.
