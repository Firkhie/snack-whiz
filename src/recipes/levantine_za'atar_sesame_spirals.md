# Levantine Za'atar Sesame Spirals

## Ingredients

### Dough
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- 1/4 cup warm water
- 1 tsp salt

### Filling
- 1/4 cup za'atar spice blend
- 1/3 cup toasted sesame seeds
- 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tbsp finely chopped fresh thyme

### Finishing
- 1 egg (for egg wash)
- Additional za'atar for sprinkling

## Instructions

1. **Prepare Dough** Mix flour, olive oil, water, and salt until a smooth, elastic dough forms. Let rest for 15 minutes.

2. **Create Filling** Combine za'atar, sesame seeds, olive oil, and thyme in a small bowl.

3. **Roll and Fill** Roll dough into a thin rectangle. Spread filling evenly across the surface.

4. **Shape Spirals** Carefully roll the dough into a tight log, then slice into 1-inch thick spirals.

5. **Bake** Place spirals on a baking sheet, brush with egg wash, and sprinkle with additional za'atar. Bake at 375°F for 20-25 minutes until golden brown and crisp.
